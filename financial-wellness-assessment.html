How your score is calculated

This assessment covers 5 areas of financial wellness with 5 questions each (25 total). Every answer is scored from 0 to 4 based on indicators aligned with CFP (Certified Financial Planner) standards — 0 reflecting the least financially healthy response and 4 the most. Your section score is the sum of your 5 answers (max 20 per section). Your overall score out of 100 is your total points expressed as a percentage of the maximum possible (100 points). Your investor profile is derived separately from 4 behavioural questions in the Savings & Investing section, which measure your attitude toward risk, loss, and time horizon.

This assessment is for educational purposes only. It is not individualized financial, tax, legal, insurance, or investment advice, and does not take into account your complete personal or financial circumstances. Results are based on your self-reported answers and should not replace personalized guidance from a licensed financial professional.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Introduction

Iberian Pacific Financial Services ("Iberian Pacific," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use and share it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights you have. We'll always be clear about how and when we collect, use, and share your personal information, and we will never sell it.

We may update this policy from time to time. If we do, we'll post the revised policy on this page. Updates are effective on the date shown above.

What Is Personal Information?

Personal information is information that, on its own or combined with other information, could identify you, for example, your name, contact details, or financial information. Where applicable, this may also include information about a spouse, family member, or beneficiary you tell us about in connection with the advice or services you're requesting.

How We Collect Your Information

We collect personal information directly from you, for example, when you:

  • Complete a contact or booking form
  • Take the Financial Wellness Assessment
  • Subscribe to our newsletter or insights emails
  • Otherwise communicate with us by phone, email, or in person

Where you've been referred to us or already work with an advisor on our team, we may also receive limited contact information about you from that advisor or from a plan sponsor, so that we can follow up with you directly.

When you visit this website, we may also collect information through cookies and similar technologies, such as which pages you visit and how you interact with the site. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

We only collect the personal information we need for the purposes described in this policy.

Types of information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Identification and contact information, such as your name, address, phone number, and email address
  • Financial information you choose to share with us, such as general income, assets, or goals, when discussing a financial plan or strategy
  • Website and Financial Wellness Assessment usage information, such as which pages you visit and, where you opt in, your assessment results

More sensitive information, such as health or medical information required to underwrite an insurance policy, is generally collected directly by the insurer or product provider administering that product, not by Iberian Pacific through this website. Where we do collect this type of information directly from you as part of arranging a product or service, we'll explain why we need it at the time.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request
  • Provide you with advice, quotes, or planning support suited to your circumstances
  • Send you newsletters or insights, where you have opted in
  • Improve our website, tools, and services
  • Meet our legal, regulatory, and compliance obligations, and help prevent fraud

Newsletter & Marketing Communications

Where you opt in to receive market insights, financial-planning content, or other updates from us, we rely on your express consent, in accordance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Consent is never pre-selected on our behalf, you must actively choose to subscribe and select the topics that interest you. Every commercial email we send identifies Iberian Pacific Financial Services and includes a way to unsubscribe. You may withdraw your consent at any time by emailing lgil@iberianpacific.ca; unsubscribe requests are honoured within 10 business days.

Cookies & Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use our site and to improve the experience over time. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Depending on the services you've asked us for, we may share information with:

  • Trusted service providers who help us operate this website and deliver our services (for example, our form-processing and email providers)
  • Customplan Financial Advisors Inc. or Monarch Wealth Corporation, where necessary to provide the insurance or investment products they distribute
  • Other insurers, investment providers, or professionals you've asked us to coordinate with on your behalf

We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect against fraud or other illegal activity. If a service provider is located outside Canada, your information may become subject to the laws of that jurisdiction.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your personal information against loss, theft, and unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Access to personal information is limited to team members and service providers who need it to do their work, and our service providers are expected to protect your information to a standard consistent with this policy. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your information protected.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Where appropriate, we may de-identify information rather than keep it in a form that could identify you.

Your Privacy Rights

You're in control of the personal information you've shared with us. Subject to certain legal exceptions, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that's inaccurate or incomplete
  • Request that we delete your personal information
  • Withdraw your consent, including unsubscribing from marketing communications, at any time
  • Ask us questions about our privacy practices

To exercise any of these rights, email info@iberianpacific.ca. We'll respond within the timeframe required by law; if we need more time, or if we're unable to fulfill a request due to a legal restriction, we'll let you know. If you withdraw consent for information we need to provide a service you've requested, we may not be able to continue providing that service.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Introduction

Iberian Pacific Financial Services ("Iberian Pacific," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you:

  • Complete a contact or booking form
  • Take the Financial Wellness Assessment
  • Subscribe to our newsletter or insights emails
  • Otherwise communicate with us by phone, email, or in person

This may include your name, email address, phone number, and any other information you choose to share with us. The Financial Wellness Assessment itself does not require personal information to complete, and your detailed answers are not transmitted to us unless you explicitly choose to share a high-level result with us (for example, when opting in to receive related insights).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request
  • Send you newsletters or insights, where you have opted in
  • Improve our website, tools, and services
  • Meet our legal, regulatory, and compliance obligations

Newsletter & Marketing Communications

Where you opt in to receive market insights, financial-planning content, or other updates from us, we rely on your express consent, in accordance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Consent is never pre-selected on our behalf — you must actively choose to subscribe and select the topics that interest you. Every commercial email we send identifies Iberian Pacific Financial Services and includes a way to unsubscribe. You may withdraw your consent at any time by emailing lgil@iberianpacific.ca; unsubscribe requests are honoured within 10 business days.

Cookies & Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use our site and to improve the experience over time. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate this website and deliver our services (for example, our form-processing and email providers), and with Customplan Financial Advisors Inc. or Monarch Wealth Corporation where necessary to provide the insurance or investment products they distribute. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time. To make a request, email info@iberianpacific.ca.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

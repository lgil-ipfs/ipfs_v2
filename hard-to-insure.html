Does any of this apply to you?

The conditions below commonly lead to rated policies, exclusions, or outright declines with standard insurers. If you've been affected by one or more of these, you're in the right place.

Cardiovascular

  • History of heart attack or cardiac event
  • Congestive heart failure
  • Atrial fibrillation or arrhythmia
  • Coronary artery disease or bypass surgery
  • Stroke or TIA (transient ischemic attack)
  • Uncontrolled hypertension

Cancer History

  • Any cancer diagnosis within the past 5–10 years
  • Certain cancers regardless of time elapsed
  • Currently in treatment or remission

Metabolic & Chronic

  • Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes (especially with complications)
  • Obesity with elevated BMI
  • Chronic kidney disease
  • Liver disease or cirrhosis
  • Crohn's disease or ulcerative colitis

Mental Health

  • Depression or anxiety with hospitalization history
  • Bipolar disorder
  • History of suicide attempt
  • Eating disorders

Neurological

  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Epilepsy or seizure disorders
  • Parkinson's disease
  • Traumatic brain injury

Respiratory

  • COPD or emphysema
  • Sleep apnea (severe or untreated)
  • Cystic fibrosis

Lifestyle & History

  • Current or recent tobacco use
  • History of substance use or addiction
  • Criminal record or high-risk occupations
  • Aviation — private pilots
  • Hazardous recreational activities

Other

  • HIV positive status
  • Organ transplant recipient
  • Autoimmune conditions (Lupus, RA, etc.)
  • Multiple prior insurance declines

"This list is not exhaustive. If your situation isn't here but you've had difficulty obtaining coverage, reach out anyway."

We go deeper on your file.

Standard brokers submit a basic application and wait. We gather your medical records, attending physician statements, and any supporting documentation that tells the full story — not just the diagnosis.

We know which insurers to approach.

Not every insurer weighs every condition the same way. Some are more favorable toward well-managed diabetes. Others are more accommodating with cancer history. We match your profile to the right market.

We advocate on your behalf.

If an initial offer comes back rated or declined, we don't stop there. We respond with evidence, request reconsideration, and where appropriate, escalate to the insurer's chief underwriter.

The Possibilities

Depending on your situation, the solution might be a standard policy with a specific exclusion, a rated policy at a higher premium, a simplified or guaranteed issue product, or a combination of coverage types that together give your family real protection.

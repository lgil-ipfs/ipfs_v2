Professional overview

Lucas is the Founder of Iberian Pacific Financial Services, an independent practice centered on institutional-grade portfolio construction, strategic investment management, and comprehensive estate planning. Built on a foundation of rigorous technical analysis and deep client advocacy, his practice provides high-net-worth individuals, families, and business owners with cohesive, full-spectrum wealth solutions.

Core areas of expertise

  • Investment & Portfolio Strategy: Custom portfolio design, asset allocation, and risk management.
  • Wealth & Retirement Planning: Comprehensive wealth accumulation, tax-efficient retirement income strategies, and cash flow optimization.
  • Risk Management & Estate Planning: Life, disability, and critical illness risk mitigation, combined with legacy and estate preservation.
  • Strategic Philanthropy: Structured charitable giving models designed to maximize community impact and tax efficiency.
  • Debt Restructuring: Targeted debt elimination frameworks and capital allocation strategies.

Professional background & education

Lucas brings a sharp quantitative foundation to wealth management, having studied mathematics and finance at the University of Waterloo, where he earned a Bachelor’s degree in Economics. His background includes extensive hands-on experience working alongside prominent Vancouver-based portfolio managers. To deliver seamless, multidisciplinary execution, he collaborates closely with trusted networks of CPAs and estate attorneys to align every tax and legal nuance with his clients’ broader financial strategies.

In addition to leading Iberian Pacific, Lucas works with the compliance and contracting team at Customplan Financial Advisors Inc. In this capacity, he conducts regulatory research, authors compliance documentation, and guides advisors through licensing and regulatory standards to ensure advisor practices remain fully compliant in an evolving regulatory environment.

Community & personal philosophy

Driven by a belief that clear financial guidance transforms lives, Lucas founded Iberian Pacific after personally coaching a close friend out of severe student debt. That hands-on experience established his lifelong commitment to debt coaching and empowerment. Today, he channels that passion into the community by volunteering his time to teach financial literacy to students and guiding clients on impactful, tax-optimized charitable giving.

Outside the office, Lucas stays active on the ice and the soccer pitch, is a dedicated collector of The Tragically Hip on CD, and brings an Anthony Bourdain–inspired curiosity to exploring the world around him.

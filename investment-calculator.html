How the comparison works

  • Same out-of-pocket savings in every account. RRSP and FHSA contributions are tax-deductible, so we assume each year's tax refund is reinvested tax-free (for example in a TFSA) and include it in the after-tax value.
  • TFSA: growth and withdrawals are tax-free, so before and after tax are the same.
  • RRSP: grows tax-deferred; withdrawals are taxed at your withdrawal tax rate.
  • FHSA: deductible like an RRSP and tax-free like a TFSA when used for a qualifying first home purchase. Contributions are capped at $8,000 a year and $40,000 in total, and the account can stay open for up to 15 years.
  • Non-registered: interest and dividends are taxed each year at today's rate, and half of the capital gain is taxed at your withdrawal rate when sold.

For illustration only. Tax rates are approximate combined federal and provincial marginal rates and do not reflect every credit, surtax, dividend tax credit, or contribution limit (TFSA and RRSP room depend on your own history). Returns are not guaranteed. Speak with an advisor and a tax professional before making decisions.

What We Design

Coverage that your team actually values — structured to control costs, maximize tax efficiency, and adapt as you grow.

Extended Health & Dental

The foundation of any group plan. We structure coverage levels that employees actually value and use — not bloated with options nobody claims.

Group Life Insurance

Basic and optional life coverage for your team, often at rates unavailable to individuals on the open market.

Group Disability

Short and long-term disability coverage keeping your employees financially stable if they can't work — protecting your team and your business continuity.

Critical Illness

Group CI coverage adds a meaningful layer of protection at a fraction of the individual cost — particularly valuable for key employees.

Health Spending Accounts (HSA)

Flexible, tax-efficient accounts allowing employees to direct dollars toward their own health priorities — reducing the employer's exposure to claims volatility.

Employee Assistance Programs

Mental health, counselling, and support resources that address what extended health doesn't cover — increasingly valued by employees at every level.

Benefits are a tax-efficient compensation tool — if structured correctly.

Premiums paid by a corporation for employee benefits are generally a deductible business expense. For incorporated professionals and small business owners, this makes a well-structured benefits plan one of the most tax-efficient ways to compensate yourself and your team. We make sure the structure reflects that.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who provides financial planning at Iberian Pacific?

Financial planning is led by Richard Chiu, QAFP, our Financial Planner. He works alongside the rest of the Iberian Pacific team, who support investment, insurance, and client service needs.

Who do you work with?

We work with individuals and families, incorporated professionals, and business owners. We also help people with complex health histories look for insurance coverage.

What is the Financial Wellness Assessment?

It's a free online questionnaire with 25 questions across five areas of your financial life. When you finish, you'll see an overall score out of 100, a breakdown by area, an investor profile, and suggested next steps. Your answers stay in your browser, and no account is required.

Is the assessment the same as a financial plan?

No. The assessment is an educational starting point that helps you reflect on your situation and identify priorities. It doesn't consider your full circumstances and isn't personalized advice. A conversation with our team is the place to talk about your specific situation.

How do I get started?

You can book a consultation online, call us at 604-916-8819, or email info@iberianpacific.ca. If you'd like to organize your thoughts first, start with the Financial Wellness Assessment.

Where is your office?

Our office is at 1090 West Georgia Street, Suite 1160, Vancouver, BC V6E 3V7.

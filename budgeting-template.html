How to Use It

Build your budget in five steps

  1. Open the template. No sign-in is needed to look around.
  2. Start with the Monthly Spending Plan. Enter your take-home pay, then list your needs, wants, and savings.
  3. Fill in your Personal Financial Snapshot. List what you own and what you owe to see your net worth.
  4. Set goals in the Goals Tracker. Add a target amount and date for each goal, like an emergency fund or a down payment.
  5. Review once a month. A short monthly check-in is usually enough to stay on track.

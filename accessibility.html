Accessibility

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Our Commitment

Iberian Pacific Financial Services is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology such as screen readers, voice control, or keyboard-only navigation.

What We Aim For

We aim to design this site in line with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), including:

  • Semantic HTML and clear page structure
  • Visible keyboard focus states on interactive elements
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background
  • Descriptive link text and alternative text for meaningful images
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences set in your browser or operating system
  • Interactive components — including the Financial Wellness Assessment, the newsletter sign-up, and site navigation — that are operable by keyboard alone

Ongoing Work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As we update and expand this website, we continue to review and improve its usability. If a page or feature does not work as expected with your assistive technology, we want to know.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, please let us know so we can address it. Contact us at info@iberianpacific.ca or 604-916-8819, and we will do our best to respond promptly.
